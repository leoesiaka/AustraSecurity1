Make a difference through professional security.

We welcome interest from people who are motivated to develop their skills and pursue opportunities within the security sector. Depending on available vacancies and assignments, roles may involve commercial premises, construction sites, residential properties or events.

We value a professional approach, sound judgement, clear communication and a shared commitment to safe working environments.

What we look for

The right attitude matters.

We encourage applications from candidates who can demonstrate the following qualities:

  • Good communication and interpersonal skills.
  • Reliability, punctuality and a responsible attitude.
  • Professional presentation and conduct.
  • Ability to remain alert and calm under pressure.
  • Respect for confidentiality, equality and diversity.
  • Willingness to follow procedures and work as part of a team.

SIA licensing and role requirements

Applicants for regulated security roles must hold the relevant, valid Security Industry Authority (SIA) licence where required for the duties involved. Additional role-specific qualifications or experience may also be necessary.

Please include your licence details and any relevant qualifications when making an enquiry, where applicable.
